Vice Frame 2 turns your own photos into bold multi-panel poster cover art in the spirit of classic game covers: five collage layouts with white keylines, five colour grades (including a true split-tone Neon Nights), and big condensed Anton lettering with a sticker badge. It ships with three original illustrated poster scenes — a sunset pier with a lowrider, a neon boulevard, and a beach day — drawn procedurally on canvas at poster resolution, alongside two bundled photographs. The app runs entirely offline in your browser and exports print-ready PNGs; your pictures never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## How it works

- **Layouts** — Vice Grid, Slash, Triptych, Mosaic and Poster arrange your pictures into keylined panels that adapt to every export aspect.
- **Pictures** — use the built-in illustrated scenes and photos, or add your own by file picker or drag & drop; click a panel to select it, drag to reposition its picture, scroll or use the slider to zoom, and deal one photo across every panel if you like.
- **Grades** — Sunset, Neon Nights (teal shadows, magenta highlights), Golden Hour, Noir and Raw are applied per panel on canvas, then finished with sunburst rays, halftone dots, a light leak, vignette and grain.
- **Caption** — two editable lines in Anton with Sunset, Neon or Chrome lettering, a size slider, top or bottom placement, an optional poster slant and a rotated sticker badge.
- **Export** — one click downloads a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080, rendered by the same pipeline as the live preview.
